# Content Studio original instrumental library

Slow Morning, Little Weekend, After Hours are original procedural instrumental recordings composed for this project with `scripts/compose_music.py`. The recordings use synthesized tones and percussion; no third-party samples or copied melodies are included.

The composition source and generated recordings are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
